# About
I'm a graduate student at the **University of Vermont** pursuing a master's in **Complex Systems & Data Science**.
I work in [The Joint Lab](https://joint-lab.github.io/) under [Laurent Hébert-Dufresne](https://laurenthebertdufresne.github.io/).
As of now, I am interested in how local interactions can give rise to complex emergent behavior. Check out my [[research]] for more specifics.

I finished my B.S. in Computer Science at UVM in 2026, and I'm now a GTA for "Modeling Complex Systems I". Highlighting my love for public speaking, I also gave a [TEDxUVM talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yozCE3uEHFA&t=0s) in January 2026.

On a more personal note, I enjoy being outdoors (especially when sailing or skiing), playing music, and collective story telling with my friends.
